Rules & keyboard

CPU: launch coral. The CPU chooses teal’s power and direction; both tops launch together.

Launch both: release to save coral’s aim and power, then prepare teal. Releasing teal starts both tops.

Power: holding reaches full power in 1 second; ripcord power depends on pull distance. A tiny tap does not launch. Escape cancels a charge.

Win: keep spinning and stay in the stadium. Leaving through an exit pocket is a ring-out; running out of spin is a spin-out. At 20 seconds, higher spin wins; under 2 percentage points apart is a draw.

Keyboard: focus the arena, use ← → to aim, hold Space to charge, and release to commit. The aim slider also works with arrows. Reset clears the round.
